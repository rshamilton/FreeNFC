NFC Forge gives you low-level access to tags, including writing memory pages, setting permanent lock bits and setting passwords. You understand and agree that:

  • Permanent lock bits can't be undone, and a tag with a lost password may be unusable.
  • You are responsible for making sure you're allowed to read, write or format any tag or card you scan.
  • NFC Forge does not bypass the encryption on secure transit or access-control cards.
