Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

In Short

  • NFC Forge doesn't collect, track, send, share or sell any of your data. That includes personal information, the contents of tags you scan, memory dumps and how you use the app. Everything happens on your iPhone.

1. What we don't collect

When you use NFC Forge, we do not collect:

  • Personal information, such as your name, email address or phone number.
  • Tag contents, including NDEF messages, contact cards, URLs and Wi-Fi passwords.
  • Raw memory dumps, lock status or tag passwords.
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, MAC address or IP address).
  • Location data.
  • Usage analytics or crash logs sent to an outside server.

2. What's stored on your iPhone

NFC Forge uses Apple's SwiftData to store things you choose to save, such as your saved tag library and the history of manual commands you've sent.

This data stays in the app's storage on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to a server or cloud service. You can delete it at any time in the app's Settings.

3. Permissions

NFC Forge only asks for what it needs to work:

  • NFC tag reading is required by iOS to talk to a tag held against your iPhone. It is only used during a scan that you start.
  • Local network access is only used if you turn on the Computer Reader in Tools. This runs a small web server on your Wi-Fi network so a browser on your Mac or PC can talk to your iPhone. Nothing is sent outside your local network.

4. Third-party code

NFC Forge contains no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. That means no Firebase, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK or ad networks.

5. Open source

NFC Forge is open source under the MIT License. Anyone can read the code at github.com/rshamilton/FreeNFC and check how it handles data.
