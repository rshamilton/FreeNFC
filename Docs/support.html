Scanning Tips

  • Use the top edgeThe iPhone's NFC antenna runs along the top of the back. Rest that edge directly on the tag.
  • Hold stillKeep the phone in place for a second or two. A full memory dump sends dozens of commands in a row.

Questions

  • Why can't I copy my transit card, credit card or office badge?

    Payment cards, transit cards (Clipper, Suica, Oyster) and most access badges are protected by hardware encryption, such as MIFARE Classic Crypto1 or a secure EMV chip. iOS doesn't give apps access to those protected sectors, so they can't be read or copied.

  • Which blank tags should I buy?

    NTAG215 (504 bytes of user memory) or NTAG213 (144 bytes) stickers and cards. They work with iPhone, Android, Amiibo and every feature in NFC Forge.

  • How do I use the Computer Reader?
    1. Put your iPhone and computer on the same Wi-Fi network.
    2. In the app, open Tools → iPhone as Computer Reader and turn the server on.
    3. On your computer, scan the QR code or type the address shown (for example http://192.168.1.50:8080) into a browser.
    4. Click "Read a tag" in the browser and hold a tag to your phone.
  • How does password recovery work?

    Open Tools → Password Protection → Recover. The app tries common factory keys (FF FF FF FF and 00 00 00 00) and any candidates you add. If one works, it resets the tag's AUTH0 page so the tag is unlocked again.

  • How do I write a Wi-Fi or contact tag?

    In the Write tab, choose Contact Card or Wi-Fi Network, fill in the fields and tap Add Record. Then tap Write to Tag and hold a blank tag to the top of your iPhone.
